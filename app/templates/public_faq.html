FAQ

Questions about Farmsource.

Where do you deliver?

Farmsource is preparing regional launches across the United States with neighborhood delivery windows.

How does delivery work?

Customers order ahead, choose a scheduled window, and drivers complete planned neighborhood routes.

Which farms participate?

Producer onboarding is in progress. The catalog will focus on local and regional sources.

Are products organic?

Producer practices will be shown as onboarding expands. Farmsource will support transparent product notes.

How are delivery windows scheduled?

Windows are set by region and order capacity so routes can be planned before delivery day.

How do drivers work?

Drivers receive assigned routes with stops in sequence and complete deliveries through the mobile web app.

How do producers join?

Producers can submit the interest form and share products, location, and operational capabilities.
