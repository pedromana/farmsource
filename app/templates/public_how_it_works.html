How it works

Scheduled farm-to-neighborhood delivery.

Farmsource organizes weekly local produce offerings, customer orders, and route-based delivery windows for participating regions nationwide.

1

Producers share availability

Farms and sources provide seasonal produce and selected products.

2

Farmsource curates

Offerings stay manageable: boxes, bundles, single produce items, and add-ons.

3

Customers schedule orders

Customers order ahead and select available neighborhood delivery windows.

4

Drivers run planned routes

Orders are packed and delivered by clustered routes, not live dispatch.
