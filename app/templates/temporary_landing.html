Local food delivery network

Fresh local produce, organized into simple scheduled delivery routes.

Farmsource Market is a nationwide farm-to-consumer marketplace building a practical way for customers to discover regional farms through scheduled neighborhood delivery routes without relying on complicated on-demand delivery.

For farms and producers

We are talking with local growers about product availability, customer demand, and whether scheduled delivery can help them reach more households.

For customers

The goal is a focused marketplace for seasonal produce, curated boxes, and farm products delivered during planned neighborhood windows.
